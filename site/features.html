What you get.

  1. 01

    A button for every repeat task

    Launch apps, run scripts, drive OBS, Spotify, Home Assistant, media keys and more — one tap each.

  2. 02

    Decks that fit the way you work

    Profiles and pages, drag to reorder, custom icons, text and colors. From idea to key in seconds.

  3. 03

    Works with your Stream Deck

    Plug in Elgato hardware and the daemon picks it up. Or go fully virtual in the browser panel.

  4. 04

    Your phone is a remote

    Open the panel from mobile on your LAN — or from anywhere through the private relay, P2P first.

  5. 05

    Local-first, light footprint

    No cloud account. A Bun + SQLite daemon idling around 100 MB — no resident Chromium in sight.

  6. 06

    Free, open, self-updating

    MIT licensed. Managed installs update from the launcher with backup and rollback — and a CLI for scripting.
